Animal Crossing N64: the original Nintendo 64 adventure, in English.
🌳 Patch your game in your browser: https://thediscordian.github.io/animal-crossing-n64/

Before the GameCube, there was Doubutsu no Mori. Come home to the game that started it all: meet your neighbours, decorate your house, write a letter, and settle into town life. Tom Nook still expects his money, of course.

This fan translation brings the familiar English script from the GameCube release to its N64 beginnings, alongside English letters, names, menus, signs, and title artwork. A custom N64-inspired keyboard makes writing your own little part of town life feel right at home.

HOW TO PLAY
Bring your own Japanese Doubutsu no Mori N64 ROM and the North American Animal Crossing GameCube disc image (USA/Canada). The website checks your files and creates the patched ROM on your device. Your game files are not uploaded, and no ROMs are provided. Supported formats, checksums, and instructions are on the patcher page.

Playing on an original N64 requires an Expansion Pak and a flash cartridge with FlashRAM and real-time clock support.
