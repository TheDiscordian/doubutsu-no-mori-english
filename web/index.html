A FEW GOOD THINGS TO KNOW

Before you move in.

Why do I need both games?

Your Japanese N64 ROM is the base. Your English GameCube disc supplies real donor data used to reconstruct the translation. Both are checked before a ROM is offered for download. No game files are supplied here.

Do my files leave my computer?

No. Your browser reads them locally and creates a new file. The only downloads are this page, its media, and the translation patch. There are no uploads, accounts, analytics, or cloud processing, and your originals are never overwritten.

Will this work with my existing town?

Saved formats are unchanged from V1 Final and the preceding V2 build. Compatibility is expected in both directions, but keep a backup. This patcher never reads or changes your saves. RC3 is not a safe fallback.

What if my file is rejected?

Use an original Japanese Doubutsu no Mori ROM and USA/Canada Animal Crossing, GAFE01 revision 0. Extract .7z/.zip archives before choosing files. Convert RVZ/GCZ/NKit to a full ISO in Dolphin. Modified, damaged, or unsupported revisions cannot be patched.

Is this the public release?

This is a local preview of the V2 patcher. The translation and N64-inspired keyboard are implemented; continued playtesting can still uncover bugs. Public patch distribution and hosting are not enabled.
