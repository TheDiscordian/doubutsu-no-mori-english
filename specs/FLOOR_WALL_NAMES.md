# Complete flooring and wallpaper names

## Approved content

Seventy explicit name approvals cover 37 flooring and 33 wallpaper entries in
`item_26` and `item_27`. The shared item-identity registry binds each complete
native spelling, source hash, supplied English slot/hash, and individual
semantic justification. Generation does not infer identity from equal indices.

The names cover established furniture-series localisations, classroom/basement/
office/kitchen floors, tatami mat counts, bamboo/steel/cowhide, mosaic tiles,
gold screens, tarpaulin, and named outdoor settings. Full supplied spelling and
capitalisation remain unchanged, including `Snowman` and `Jingle`.

Native floor rows `000D/000E/000F` mean log house, cracked road, and lunar
surface. Their complete references are cabin rug, closed road, and lunar
surface. The legacy glosses are displaced and cannot establish these identities.
Native rows `0013/0014` retain green and blue, respectively; neither inherits
the shifted legacy regal/ranch names. Native wall `0001` is the chic/classic
series. Native wall `0000` is a different pattern and is not newly approved.

Both groups' `0012` bath/bathhouse designs and `001A` old wood/earth designs
remain unapproved: the supplied same-index western landscape and backyard
names do not describe those native settings. Other ambiguous species, materials,
patterns, and design changes remain in the generated remaining reports. This
name batch does not replace any texture or assert identical artwork.

## Storage and integration

All seventy complete names fit the existing sixteen-byte item resource. Ten
also fit the unchanged ten-byte native fields; the other sixty are not shortened
or copied into smaller buffers. The wider resource contains 2,783 translated
slots. Complete ordinary item candidates occupy 789 native slots. The previous
2,713 wider and 779 ordinary candidates retain their exact content and provenance.

The resource header, VROM, byte size, entry count, native item identities, and
all caller capacities remain unchanged. The complete ROM must retain every
non-item edit, module, font, mail resource, and patch; only item-name bank data
and the existing wider item resource may differ from the preceding glyph build.
Existing wider item insertion uses complete names. Unexpanded inventory,
catalogue, handbill, and editor destinations remain integration work, not a
reason to shorten names in this batch.

## Acceptance

Tests check all seventy source/reference identities, retain earlier candidates,
reject rehashed shortening, and retain the explicit design exclusions. Resource
reconstruction must match installed data. An independent comparison checks every
old edit, every new full name, every DMA file, and application of the generated
UPS to the original ROM. The native batch reads both complete wide names and
the ten new unchanged-width entries with adjacent guards and checkpoint restore.
Normal item displays, remaining wider callers, final presentation, save/reload,
and original hardware remain project acceptance work.

Exact build/test results and current limitations belong in
[the checkpoint](../docs/checkpoints/FLOOR_WALL_NAMES.md).
