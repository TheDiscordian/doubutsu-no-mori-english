# Item-name application boundary

## Installed family

`tools/item_name_readers.py` enables resource-only translation credit when all
required display and letter integrations are installed. Every required route
receives actual installed-code/resource verification during measurement; a flag
alone does not establish application. Earlier partial builds retain pending
credit. Untranslated resource rows remain untranslated even when all readers
are complete, including the accented-name queue.

## Shared fields and non-item inputs

`build/audits/item-fields.json` records 27 item-field setter calls, two insertion
calls, five item-ID wrapper calls, no direct copy-out getter calls, and no aligned
literal pointers to those four APIs. Main-window item storage is five complete
sixteen-byte rows with native ten-byte mirrors; all setters replace/clear the
whole owned row. Main dialogue uses the full insertion routine. The complete
dynamic-choice entry bypasses the old ten-byte choice insertion and calls the
same full routine with bounded staging. General free-item fields use the twenty
sixteen-byte startup rows and complete plain/coloured insertion.

The other recorded setters do not load item-bank names. Ordinary residents use
random-word strings, numeric words, and constructed shop text; their relevant
word integrations are separate. Katrina uses its complete sixteen-byte phrase
integration. Resetti uses apology strings, not item IDs. Sample-actor inputs are
embedded diagnostic strings. The song-request echo is saved user-entered text;
the selected song title has its own complete item-ID route. These inputs retain
their own source IDs and unresolved work; completing item-name application does
not credit any of them as translated.

The five original item-ID wrapper callers use the installed full resident
entry. Its main setter, wrapper, and dialogue insertion instructions are checked
explicitly. Catalogue and song routes receive additional verification beyond
their previous bank-only accounting. The complete persistent world-font image,
configuration, and startup hooks are validated through the font and notice
dependencies. All other tabled routes use their existing whole installed
integration verifiers in `translation_progress.measure`.

This closes the inventoried item-name readers, not accented glyph application,
all interface strings, custom input expansion, arbitrary indirect references,
or exhaustive gameplay. Newly discovered untranslated readers reopen the gate.
